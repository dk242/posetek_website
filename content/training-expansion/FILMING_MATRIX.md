## Production standard

- Primary demo: complete correct movement at normal speed, stable framing and both sides when relevant.
- Teaching detail: clear apparatus setup, contact points, the main cue and an easier option.
- Error correction: explain one recognizable mistake; demonstrate only a safe low-effort contrast and finish with correct movement.
- Record original demonstrations. Do not reuse research/publication media or claim medical treatment, guaranteed test gains or a completed qualified review.
- Review technique, age/experience eligibility, supervision, dose, wording and all three clips before publishing. Empty media fields are intentional.

### wb-001: Goblet squat

**Week1; strength.** Develops a physical capacity that can support the linked performance domain as part of a complete program.

Setup: Stand on a clear level floor holding the coach-selected kettlebell at the chest. Practise the unloaded pattern first.

1. Set feet in a comfortable stance and brace gently.
2. Sit between the hips while keeping the whole foot grounded.
3. Stand smoothly and finish tall without leaning back.

Dose for review: 1-2 sets x 6-8 reps; 60-90 seconds between sets. Draft coaching range, not an athlete prescription. Coach approves the dose, load and total weekly work. Stop before technique changes.

Easier: Unloaded squat to a comfortable depth. Progression: Coach increases the load only after consistent depth and control.

Distinct from existing content: Adds external anterior loading and an unsupported squat after the existing box competency drill; not another box-height variant. Compare STR-001.

Evidence: consensusExtrapolation; support. [NSCA2009](https://dxpprod.nsca.com/globalassets/about/position-statements/position_stand_youth_resistance_training---2009.pdf), [AAP2020](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article-pdf/145/6/e20201011/1078888/peds_20201011.pdf), [NEGRA2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27050241/). The named exercise, variation and dose were not isolated in the cited trials. Linking a domain is a coaching inference, not diagnosis of a weakness or a guaranteed test improvement.

**Primary demo:** Show goblet squat from setup to finish at normal speed. Keep the whole body and equipment visible. Set feet in a comfortable stance and brace gently. Sit between the hips while keeping the whole foot grounded. Stand smoothly and finish tall without leaning back. Film both sides where relevant; show a reset rather than a fatigue set.

**Teaching detail:** Show the setup close enough to reproduce it: Stand on a clear level floor holding the coach-selected kettlebell at the chest. Practise the unloaded pattern first. Explain: Keep the bell close and knees following the toes. Demonstrate the easier option: Unloaded squat to a comfortable depth. Explain that the coach chooses progression and any external load.

**Error correction:** Explain the error without deliberately loading an unsafe position: Heels lifting: reduce depth and practise without the bell. Contrast a safe low-effort example with the correct version. Refilm normal correct repetitions. Do not demonstrate pain, maximal strain, risky falls or unsafe equipment use.
