# Copy rules (guide §8 + workbook README; binding for all athlete-facing text)

Source: PoseTek Drill Matrix Research & Implementation Guide v1.0 (2026-08-02). Validators
spot-check these; the model must follow them in every generated plan, workout, and report.

## Causal language

Pose features are clues, never proven causes. Say **"may be limiting"** or **"is associated
with"**. Never say a joint angle, posture, or pose feature **"caused"** a result. Recommend
from a repeated pattern across valid trials — never one clip or one joint angle.

## Use / avoid pairs

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| "Your acceleration is below your matched comparison range." | "You are slow." |
| "Your more upright first steps may be limiting early acceleration." | "Your trunk angle caused the result." |
| "This change is within normal test variation, so we can't call it an improvement yet." | Celebrating a change smaller than device noise |
| "No closely matched benchmark is available; showing your personal trend." | A pooled percentile presented as precise |
| "Pain or sudden movement avoidance needs human review before progression." | Any injury diagnosis or prevention guarantee |

## Change classification

Only three states — **Improved / Unclear / Declined** — and only after comparing the change
against protocol noise. Show a confidence level (High / Moderate / Low), and stay conservative
while device reliability data is missing.

## Evidence honesty

Evidence tiers ride each drill: A = synthesis/consensus, B = direct study or governing-body
guidance, C = evidence-informed implementation not individually validated. Tier C drills must
not be presented as "proven". The evidence supports the training method and dosage family, not
each named drill individually.

## Youth framing

- No performance labels for U6–U8; score personal bests and quality.
- Never equate maturity advantage with talent; do not lock young players to positions.
- Strength/resilience work is presented as development options and availability-supporting
  habits — never treatment, injury prevention guarantees, or medical advice.
- The app is not a medical diagnostic system and never claims to be.

## Disclosure

While `drillCatalogMeta/current.contentReviewed` is false, plans must carry a disclaimer that
drill content is evidence-informed but pending expert review.
